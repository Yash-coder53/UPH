ABOUT

What is User Protector Hub?

User Protector Hub is an independent safety initiative that helps Telegram users report serious violations — including CSAM, sexual violence, coordinated abuse, and credible threats — using evidence-based methods.

Evidence-Based

Every report requires verifiable proof — screenshots, message links, or media. We do not act on rumours.

Transparent Process

You decide how your report is submitted and can see exactly what is sent. Nothing is filed without your confirmation.

Community Protection

Focused entirely on serious harms: child sexual abuse material, sexual violence, and credible threats.

PROCESS

How It Works

1

Start the Bot

Open our Telegram bot and select a violation category.

2

Provide Target

Share the username, channel link, or message URL.

3

Submit Evidence

Upload screenshots, videos, or text proof.

4

Choose Method

Submit via the Telegram API or email to Trust & Safety.

TERMS OF USE

Usage Guidelines

  • Use only for genuine, serious violations.
  • Always provide evidence (screenshots, links).
  • Do not use for harassment, revenge, or false reports.
  • Respect Telegram's Terms of Service at all times.
  • Misuse may result in a permanent ban from the tool.

Disclaimer: User Protector Hub is an independent initiative and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Telegram Messenger LLP.

DATA HANDLING

Privacy & Data

We do not store your personal data beyond the duration of your report. Evidence files are deleted after submission. No tracking, no ads, no third-party analytics.
